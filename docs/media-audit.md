# CCDI media audit

Reviewed 235 source images and videos: 223 images and 12 videos. Contact sheets were used to inspect every photograph; four frames across each video were inspected. Native browser playback was also verified.

The web library contains 123 selected image assets and all 12 videos. Originals remain unchanged in `images/`. Delivery images are WebP; delivery videos use H.264/AAC with fast-start metadata and poster frames.

## Placement decisions

- Home: catering instruction, baking trainees, enterprise catering, handmade work, and family events.
- Initiatives: matching photographs and programme films for skills, enterprise, welfare, and community work.
- Training cards: fashion, bakery, beauty, agriculture, and publication visuals. The ICT card uses a factual training-centre photograph because no clearly identifiable ICT session was supplied.
- Gallery: the reviewed photographs, with category filters, progressive loading and full-image previews.
- Team: photographs from the supplied management/facilitator folder; unnamed people are not assigned identities.
- Resources: publication covers and programme flyers; historical flyers are explicitly presented as archive material.
- Videos: all 12 supplied clips, with controls, no autoplay, poster frames and on-demand loading.

## Duplicate handling

Filenames are never used to identify duplicates. Byte hashes compare actual file contents. Visually similar images were reviewed in contact sheets; alternate crops, collages and frames are not automatically declared identical. A representative image was chosen for repeated scenes, while distinct scenes and useful formats were retained. No original file was removed.

## Video accessibility

Players have descriptive titles and native controls. The source folder does not provide caption tracks or verified transcripts; these have not been fabricated.
